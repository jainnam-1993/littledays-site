Privacy Policy

Last Updated: March 5, 2026  ·  Effective Date: March 5, 2026

1. Introduction

LittleDays ("we," "our," or "us") provides an AI-powered communication platform for home daycares and parents ("Service"). This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, disclose, and safeguard your information when you use our mobile application.

By using LittleDays, you agree to the collection and use of information in accordance with this policy. If you do not agree with this policy, please do not use the Service.

2. Information We Collect

2.1 Account Information

  • Staff/Admin accounts: Name, email address, daycare name
  • Parent accounts: Name, email address

2.2 Child Information

Daycare administrators enter the following information about children in their care:

  • Child's first name
  • Date of birth
  • Allergies and dietary restrictions
  • Classroom or group assignment
Important: Children do not use LittleDays directly. All child information is entered and managed by authorized daycare administrators. Parents view information about their own children only.

2.3 Daily Activity Data

  • Daily update notes written by daycare staff (meals, naps, activities, mood)
  • Incident and accident reports
  • Supply count updates
  • Photos taken by daycare staff during the day

2.4 Device and Usage Information

  • Device type and operating system
  • Push notification tokens (Firebase Cloud Messaging)
  • App usage patterns (screens visited, features used)

2.5 Payment Information

Subscription payments are processed through RevenueCat and the respective app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store). We do not directly collect or store credit card numbers or payment credentials. Please refer to RevenueCat's privacy policy and your app store's privacy policy for details on payment data handling.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide the Service: Deliver daily updates, incident reports, photos, and supply notifications between daycare staff and parents
  • AI-Powered Summaries: Process daily update notes through AI (OpenAI API) to generate parent-friendly summaries of a child's day. Only the text of daily notes and the child's first name are sent to the AI service; no other personal information is transmitted
  • Push Notifications: Send timely notifications to parents about daily updates, incident reports, and photos via Firebase Cloud Messaging
  • Account Management: Manage user accounts, authentication, and subscription status
  • Service Improvement: Understand how the Service is used to improve features and user experience

4. Data Retention

We retain data for limited periods to minimize exposure:

Data Type Retention Period
Daily updates24 hours after creation
Photos24 hours after upload
Incident reports30 days after creation
Account informationUntil account deletion is requested
Subscription dataManaged by RevenueCat per their retention policy

After the retention period expires, data is automatically and permanently deleted from our systems.

5. Data Storage and Security

  • Database: All structured data (accounts, updates, reports) is stored in Supabase, a cloud-hosted PostgreSQL database with row-level security (RLS) policies that restrict data access based on user role and association
  • Photo Storage: Photos are stored in Supabase Storage with signed URLs that expire after the retention period
  • Encryption: Data is encrypted in transit (TLS/SSL) and at rest
  • Access Control: Role-based access ensures parents see only their own children's data, and staff see only data for their daycare

6. AI Processing

LittleDays uses AI (via OpenAI's API) to rewrite daily update notes into warm, parent-friendly summaries. When this occurs:

  • Only the raw text of the daily note and the child's first name are sent to the AI service
  • No photos, account details, dates of birth, or other personal identifiers are sent
  • AI-generated summaries are stored alongside the original notes and follow the same 24-hour retention policy
  • We do not use your data to train AI models. Our use of OpenAI's API is governed by OpenAI's data usage policies for API customers, which do not use API inputs for model training

8. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

LittleDays is designed for use by adults (daycare staff and parents). Children do not create accounts or interact with the app directly.

  • No direct collection from children: All child-related information is entered by authorized daycare administrators
  • Parental access: Parents can view all information stored about their child through the app
  • Parental control: Parents may request deletion of their child's data by contacting their daycare administrator or by using the account deletion feature
  • Limited data: We collect only the minimum child information necessary to provide the Service (first name, date of birth, allergies)
  • No advertising: We do not display advertisements or use children's information for marketing

If you believe we have inadvertently collected information directly from a child under 13, please contact us immediately so we can delete it.

9. Your Rights and Choices

9.1 Access and Correction

You can view and update your account information within the app's Settings screen.

9.2 Account Deletion

You may request deletion of your account and all associated data through the Settings screen in the app. Upon deletion:

  • Your account and personal information will be permanently removed
  • Any data already past its retention period will have been automatically deleted
  • Active data (within retention windows) will be deleted immediately
  • Subscription will be cancelled through your app store

9.3 Push Notification Preferences

You can disable push notifications at any time through your device's system settings or within the app.

9.4 Data Export

You may request a copy of your data by contacting us at the email address below.

10. California Privacy Rights

If you are a California resident, you have the right to:

  • Know what personal information is collected about you
  • Request deletion of your personal information
  • Not be discriminated against for exercising your privacy rights

To exercise these rights, use the account deletion feature in the app or contact us at the email below.
