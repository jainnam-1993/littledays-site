How to Get Help

To help us resolve your issue as quickly as possible, please include the following when you write to us:

  • The email address associated with your LittleDays account
  • Whether you are a daycare admin, staff member, or parent
  • A brief description of the issue you're experiencing
  • The device and operating system you're using (e.g., iPhone 15, iOS 17)
  • Any screenshots that might help illustrate the problem

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I create a parent account?

Parent accounts are created via an invite link sent by your daycare administrator. Ask your daycare to send you an invite. You cannot sign up independently as a parent.

I didn't receive a push notification. What's wrong?

Check that notifications are enabled for LittleDays in your device's Settings. On iPhone, go to Settings → LittleDays → Notifications. On Android, go to Settings → Apps → LittleDays → Notifications.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed through your app store. On iPhone, go to Settings → [Your Name] → Subscriptions. On Android, open the Google Play Store → tap your profile → Payments & subscriptions → Subscriptions.

How do I delete my account?

You can delete your account from within the app. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account. This will permanently remove your account and all associated data. For child data deletion, please also contact your daycare administrator.

Why did my photos disappear?

Photos in LittleDays are automatically deleted after 24 hours as part of our privacy-first design. This is intentional — we retain the minimum data necessary. If you'd like to keep a photo, save it to your device before the 24-hour window closes.

I have a privacy concern or a COPPA question

Privacy questions can be directed to privacy@littledays.app. If you believe we have inadvertently collected information directly from a child under 13, contact us immediately and we will delete it.

The AI summary seems wrong — what should I do?

AI-generated summaries are meant to assist, not replace, daycare staff judgment. Staff should review summaries before sending them to parents. If something seems off, the staff member can edit the summary before it goes out. AI errors should be reported to your daycare admin.
